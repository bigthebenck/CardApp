    How the shuffles work

    Overhand run: run X cards singly off the top (1–52; 52 reverses the deck).

    Packet run: pick up the top X cards (2–52), run Y of them one at a time onto the deck and drop the rest on top. Y “all” reverses the top X.

    Cut: move the top X cards (1–51) to the bottom.

    Partial faro: cut off X cards (up to 26) from the top or bottom and weave them into the top or bottom of the rest. Out keeps the packet’s outer card on the outside; in tucks it one card inside.

    Split A|B: split so A is the card you see on the bottom of the upper packet and B is the top card of the rest.

      How the steps work

      Piles are lettered: the deck starts as pile A, and each new pile takes the next letter. Shuffles, splits and added cards go to the pile picked above.

      Take out named cards from wherever they are: as one new pile, a pile each, or put away for good. Add cards puts new ones in (X5 adds five any cards).

      Split a pile into packets from the top, e.g. 1,1,1,1,12,12,12,12; cards left over make one more packet. Put pile one pile on top of or under another. Gather stacks piles into one, the first on top.

      Name X cards says which cards some indifferent cards are, in earlier steps too, e.g. to know the card at a faro's split. Rearrange types the whole new order, e.g. after a trick.

      Groups: open a step and tap Group… to title and colour a run of steps, such as one trick of a routine. Tap a group's heading to edit, copy or ungroup it.
